      how to play

      cover — guess the Feng release from its cover. it starts as 1 pixel, then 2×2, 4×4, 8×8, 16×16, 32×32.

      song — guess the song from a clip. you get 0.5s, then 1s, 3s, then 5s (capped).

      easy plays the hook. hard plays a random part of the song instead — never the chorus. hard has its own daily puzzle and stats.

      in unlimited, press enter after a round to jump to the next one.

      6 tries. skipping uses a try.

      close: same project (song) / same type (cover)

      daily resets at midnight. unlimited is endless practice with its own separate stats.
